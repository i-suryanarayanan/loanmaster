Common questions

Things people ask before calling

Do you give the loan yourselves?

No. Loan Master is a loan advisory and registered DSA. The loan is always sanctioned and disbursed by the bank or NBFC. We help you find the right one, prepare the file correctly and follow it through.

Can you guarantee my loan will be approved?

No, and you should be careful of anyone who promises that. Approval is the bank's decision. What we can do is check your eligibility honestly beforehand and point you where your chances are strongest — which is why our files rarely get rejected.

My CIBIL score is low. Is it pointless?

Not necessarily. Some lenders weigh income and existing relationships more heavily, and in many cases a few months of small corrections lift the score enough to qualify. Talk to us first rather than applying and collecting rejections.

What do you charge?

Nothing upfront, ever. The enquiry, eligibility check, bank comparison and paperwork are all free. A minimal service fee is payable only once the loan is sanctioned and disbursed — and the amount is told to you in advance. If the loan does not come through, there is no charge at all.

I live outside Thrissur. Can you still help?

Yes. Most of the process runs over WhatsApp and phone, and we work with customers across Kerala. NRIs are welcome too — we handle the coordination here while you're abroad.

Is my personal information safe?

Your documents are used only for the applications you approve, and shared only with the lender you select. We don't sell contact details or pass your number on to anyone else.
